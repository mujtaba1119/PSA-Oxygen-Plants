Privacy Policy — OxyTrack

Last updated: 19 August 2026

OxyTrack ("the App") is a monitoring and ticketing tool used by authorized staff to track the status of PSA oxygen plants and related equipment at hospitals. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used. The App is operated by the individual owner of OxyTrack ("we", "us").

Information We Collect

The App is intended for authorized users (hospital staff, plant operators, and administrators) and collects only the information needed to operate the service:

  • Account information: your username, display name, assigned role, and organization, used to sign you in and show you the correct information.
  • Ticket and comment content: the issue reports, comments, status updates, and dates you submit within the App.
  • Photos and attachments: if you choose to attach photos or documents to a ticket, the App accesses your camera or photo library to upload the files you select. The App only accesses images you explicitly choose to attach.
  • Push notification token: a device token used solely to deliver notifications about tickets and site status to your device.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide the service: to authenticate users, display and manage tickets and site status, deliver notifications, and allow authorized users to communicate about equipment issues. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Camera and Photo Access

The App requests camera and photo access only so you can attach images of equipment issues to a ticket. Access is used at the moment you choose to add an attachment. The App does not access, scan, or upload images you do not explicitly select.

Data Storage and Security

Information is stored securely on managed cloud infrastructure (including Supabase and Vercel) and transmitted over encrypted connections. Access is limited to authorized users and administrators of the service.

Data Sharing

Information is shared only among authorized users of the service as required for its function (for example, a ticket raised by a hospital is visible to the relevant service providers and administrators). We do not share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use trusted service providers (such as cloud hosting and push-notification delivery) solely to operate the App.

Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the service and maintain records of equipment issues. Account information is removed when an account is deleted by an administrator.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for authorized adult staff and is not directed to children.

Your Choices

You may decline camera, photo, or notification permissions in your device settings; some features may not function without them. To request removal of your account or data, contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
